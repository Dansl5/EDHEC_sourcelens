Privacy

What we collect, and what we do not.

What we collect

We use cookieless visit statistics (GoatCounter). They show pages viewed, approximate country and browser type.

There are no cookies and no cross-site tracking.

Sign-up forms

In this prototype stage, form entries are not stored or sent anywhere.

Submissions are only counted anonymously in our visit statistics.

Claim checker

Pasted text goes to Google's Gemini API, with Google Search, to produce the verdict. We don't store it; Google may keep it under its API terms. Don't paste personal data.
