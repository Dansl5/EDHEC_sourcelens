## 1. Riskiest assumption (Lean Canvas: Customer Segments, Problem, Revenue Streams)
**Main test A:** citizens who meet a viral political claim will sign up for a pre-share checker, and some will pick Premium. **Supporting test B:** newsrooms and fact-checkers, the economic buyer, will accept a free concierge pilot.

## 2. Hypotheses
**A.** We believe that a landing page with an instant claim-checker demo and free waitlist, for EU social-media users aged 18–35 who share political news, will get them to sign up. We will know this is true when at least 10% of unique visitors join the waitlist (T1) and at least 3% click "Start 30-day free trial" (T2).

**B.** We believe that a free concierge pilot for regional newsrooms and fact-checkers will get them to take part. We will know this is true when at least 3 (T3) of 20 (N) contacted organisations reply positively or book a call.

## 3. Method
Fake-door and waitlist test on the live page; six channels, each with its own tracking link; 6–8 hallway tests. A unique visitor is one GoatCounter session; team visits, tests and duplicate emails are excluded. Premium says "not live yet" and takes no payment.

## 4. Pass/fail rule (written before any data)
**Metric that matters: share of unique visitors who join the waitlist (T1).** A threshold passes if the observed rate meets it; 95% Wilson intervals are shown. **Persevere:** T1 passes and T2 or T3 passes. **Adjust offer and price:** T1 passes, T2 and T3 fail. **Refocus on newsrooms:** T1 fails, T3 passes. **Pivot to platforms (API):** T1 and T3 fail.
