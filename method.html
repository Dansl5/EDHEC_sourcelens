How SourceLens checks a claim

Short answers on verdicts, sources and appeals.

What a verdict means

  • True: reliable sources clearly confirm it.
  • False: reliable sources clearly contradict it.
  • Misleading: partly true, overstated, or missing key context.
  • Not yet verified: no reliable source found, or not a checkable factual claim.

Where sources come from

Every verdict links the sources it is based on.

Sources come from the search results the checker used. They never come from links it wrote itself.

No source means no True or False verdict.

What we never do

  • We never delete or hide posts.
  • We never rate opinions, parties or candidates.
  • We take no government or party funding.

AI and its limits

Verdicts are produced by an AI model that searches the web.

It can be wrong. Check the sources before you share.

Appeals

Anyone can ask us to review a verdict.

Email hello@sourcelens.eu with the claim and why you think the verdict is wrong.

A person reviews every appeal.
