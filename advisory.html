Official announcement · Security advisory

Customer login credentials were leaked online

Published 29 October 2026 · Updated as the investigation progresses

Today we learned that email addresses and passwords for some SourceLens accounts have been posted online. We have reset every account password as a precaution and are working with independent security experts to find out exactly which accounts are affected and how the data was obtained. Your fact-check history and the verdicts we publish remain unchanged. We are sorry. A service built on trust has to earn it back, and we will report openly on every step.

What you should do now

  1. Set a new password the next time you open the app or extension.
  2. Change your password on any other site where you used the same one.
  3. Turn on two-step verification in your account settings.
  4. Ignore emails or messages asking for your password. SourceLens will never ask for it.

Newsrooms and API customers

  • All API keys have been revoked. New keys are in your dashboard.
  • Review your dashboard access log for unfamiliar logins.
  • Your account manager will call you today.

Questions: security@sourcelens.example
