# Hallway test script (5 minutes, 6 to 8 people)

Use a phone with the live page open (or the QR code in `qr-hallway.png`). Do not explain the product first. Write down answers word for word.

1. **10-second look.** Show the page for 10 seconds, then hide it. Ask: "What does this do? Who is it for?"
2. **Try the checker.** "Try the checker with the 'Vote by text' example. What did you expect to happen?"
3. **Install.** "Would you install it? What would stop you?"
4. **Price.** "Would you pay 3.99 euros a month for unlimited checks? Why or why not?"
5. **Buyer.** "Who do you think pays for this?"
